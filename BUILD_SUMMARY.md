# Doctor's Dental Care Website - Build Summary

## Project Completion Status: ✓ COMPLETE

A premium, production-ready dental clinic website has been successfully built for Doctor's Dental Care in Savar, Bangladesh.

## What Was Built

### 1. Foundation & Global Components
- **React Router Navigation**: Multi-page SPA with 7 main routes
- **Header Component**: Sticky navigation with responsive mobile menu
- **Footer Component**: Comprehensive footer with contact info, quick links, and social media
- **Floating CTAs**: Fixed WhatsApp and Call buttons for easy customer contact
- **Global CSS**: Professional styling system with CSS variables for theming

### 2. Home Page (Complete)
1. **Hero Section** - Eye-catching banner with brand message and CTAs
2. **Why Choose Us** - 8 benefit cards highlighting clinic strengths
3. **Services Grid** - 9 service categories with visual icons (6 treatments per category)
4. **Doctor Cards** - 3 expert dentist profiles with qualifications
5. **Testimonials** - Patient reviews with ratings and statistics
6. **Appointment Form** - Multi-field booking form with success confirmation
7. **FAQ Accordion** - 10 general clinic FAQs with smooth expand/collapse
8. **Contact Information** - Hours, address, phone, email

### 3. Services System
- **Services Listing Page** - Filterable by 9 categories with sidebar navigation
- **Dynamic Service Pages** - 28 individual service detail pages including:
  - Service description and benefits (1500+ word template)
  - Step-by-step procedures
  - Recovery and aftercare information
  - Pricing and cost factors
  - Service-specific FAQs (15-20 items per service)
  - Related services suggestions
  - Booking CTAs

### 4. Team Page
- Full profiles of 3 BMDC-registered dentists
- Credentials and registration numbers
- Availability schedules
- Services and specializations
- Experience summary
- Team statistics and success rates

### 5. About Page
- Clinic mission and values
- History and background
- 8 key differentiators
- Contact information and hours
- Professional presentation

### 6. Contact Page
- Full contact details and address
- Interactive contact information
- Operating hours with emergency notice
- Social media links
- Map location reference
- Appointment booking CTA

### 7. Additional Pages
- **404 Page** - Professional error page with home link
- **Service Detail Pages** - Dynamic routing for all 28 services
